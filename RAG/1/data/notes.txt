Moisture and odor in PP staple handling:
If PP staple tow is transferred or baled while still wet, trapped moisture can stay inside the bale.
Trapped moisture can create a “wet laundry” type smell over time, even if the finish includes a biocide, because the damp environment can promote odor formation.
High moisture can also increase fiber-to-fiber sticking and reduce good separation during bale opening, making opening more difficult.
Allowing adequate drying time (or reducing water carryover in finish dilution) helps reduce odor complaints and improves opening consistency.
